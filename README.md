#Paseo Ecommerce Profit Dashboard – Power BI Visualization
This Power BI dashboard provides a detailed view of ecommerce profit data using various charts, slicers, and metrics. It helps track profit trends, analyze product discounts, and understand sales distribution across different countries.

📊 Dashboard Components & Their Insights 

1️⃣ Profit Summary Card (Top Center)

This large metric card displays the total profit as 4.80M (4,800,000).
It gives a quick overview of overall earnings.

2️⃣ Slicers (Top Right)

Segment Slicer: Allows users to filter the data by different business segments.
Country Slicer: Lets users choose specific countries to analyze.

3️⃣ Profit by Month (Top Left – Waterfall Chart)

This waterfall chart shows monthly profit trends.
Blue bars indicate an increase in profit, while red bars (not visible) would represent a decrease.
The total profit increases steadily each month, showing consistent growth.
The highest profit months appear to be towards the later months.

4️⃣ Profit by Country (Top Right – Pie Chart)

This pie chart breaks down total profit by country.
Top contributing countries:
Canada (1.27M – 26.37%)
United States (1.02M – 21.27%)
Germany (0.93M – 19.36%)
Mexico (0.74M – 15.52%)
France (0.93M – 19.36%)
Canada has the highest profit share.

5️⃣ Count of Products by Discount Band (Bottom Left – Bar Chart)

This horizontal bar chart categorizes products based on discount levels.
The highest number of products fall under the High discount category.
Medium & Low discounts also have good sales, while None has the least count.
This suggests that higher discounts increase sales.

6️⃣ Profit Data Table (Bottom Right)

A detailed table displays:
Month Name
Segment (Government, Midmarket)
Country
Discount Band (Low, Medium, High)
Product (Paseo)
Sum of Profit
The total sum of profit (4,797,437.95) aligns with the profit card.
🛠 Key Takeaways
✅ Profits have increased consistently over the months.
✅ Canada contributes the most to total profit.
✅ Higher discounts lead to more product sales.
✅ The dashboard is interactive – users can filter data using slicers.
